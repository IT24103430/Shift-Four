## Contracts to preserve

- **Perception:** camera evidence must have its own witness, position-at-observation, age and expiry semantics. A camera alarm is not automatically a gunshot or the drone's own visual confirmation. Define external-observation authority explicitly; preserve existing own-vision prediction history and do not read a hidden target Transform to refresh it. Camera-only awareness must not bypass normal firing checks.
- **Lifecycle:** subscriptions register once and clean up on disable/destroy/restart and domain-reload-disabled Play entry. A destroyed camera or old scene must not keep updating current drones. Adding camera Health must not accidentally count it as one of the four drone objectives.
- **Ambush feasibility:** every post needs complete native/gate-aware routes and full raised-body clearance, bounded commitment, timeout and a recovery path. Use the existing validators. `DroneMotor.Stop()` is terminal death/shutdown behavior; it is not a resumable ambush pause.
- **Gate identity:** gate `i` requires console `i` AND drone `i` defeated. A living drone cannot ambush at the opening of its own exit gate under this contract. Next-room defenders may be relevant: Guard at gate 0, Interceptor at gate 1, Flanker at gate 2. Gate 3 leads to the exit after all four defenders are defeated. Any alternative requires an explicit mission-design decision. Never silently weaken prerequisites.
- **Gate physics:** do not move the stationary physical root or release its collider/carving before the bolt/panel animation finishes. Native carving settles asynchronously. Do not add automatic closing onto the player without a separately approved physical contract.
- **Damage compatibility:** current enemy shots are 10 damage, 11 m range and 1.1 s cooldown; player shots are 25 damage/.24 s. New profile fields need explicit backward-compatible defaults/validation for existing assets. Preserve accepted-hit VFX, attacker/reason/fatal records, recovery and death objective identity. Do not call `Health.Configure` on an active life to refresh tuning; it heals/resets a life.
- **Flight coordinates:** keep the planar NavMesh root and current route/observation assumptions unless the actual brief deliberately changes them. Compose altitude once in the existing visual pose owner; do not add competing Transform writers. Update collider/envelope, muzzle/vision origin and tactical clearance coherently with any visual height change. Preserve authored model height, camera clearance and roof clearance.
- **Fatal priority:** altitude/scan/ambush controllers must release authority immediately on death. The existing wreck rig owns gravity, collision, impact and settling; no alive controller may pull it back to hover.
- **Assets:** no scene regeneration, broad prefab replacement or GUID recreation. Existing section/agent prefabs, MainLab, navigation data, models and materials remain owner-gated. Prefer targeted Editor property operations after approval.
